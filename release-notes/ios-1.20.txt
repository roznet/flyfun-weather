- The briefing toolbar is simpler: Start/Stop on flight day, Refresh, and one ⋯ menu for Share, Notifications, Edit/Duplicate and feedback. Edit and Duplicate stay visible, greyed out, when you're offline instead of disappearing.
- The forecast map has a labelled Forecast | Past switch, solid controls that stay readable over any map, and a single time bar for day and hour. A hide toggle clears the controls when you want more map.
- The briefing's route map gets the same layout. Metrics are grouped into "At altitude" and "Along route", and there's a Fit route button and a direct link to the forecast map. Route segments with no data now show grey instead of vanishing.
- When a briefing has no GRAMET, the app now says why. After linking Autorouter, a refresh fetches it.
- During server maintenance or an outage, the app shows a clear banner instead of a raw server error.
- The More menu on the flight list is reorganised: Website and Settings first, then What's New, Help and Send Feedback.
